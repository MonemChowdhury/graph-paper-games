Graph Paper Games – Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 14, 2026 · Developer: Monem Shariar Chowdhury

Graph Paper Games ("the app") is a set of offline and online board games. This policy explains what happens to information when you use it.

Information we collect

We do not collect, store, sell or share any personal information. The app has no accounts, no sign-in, no advertising, no analytics and no tracking. Game scores stay on your device and are cleared when you close the game.

Online play

When you choose Online with a friend, the app connects your device directly to your friend's device using WebRTC. To introduce the two devices, the app briefly contacts a public connection service run by the open-source PeerJS project (0.peerjs.com). That service sees a random game code and, like any internet service, your device's IP address while connecting. It does not receive your name, contacts, location or game history, and the developer of this app does not receive or store any of this information. Only game moves (for example "square 5") are sent between the two devices, and nothing is saved after the game ends.

If you do not want to connect to this service, play against the computer or with a friend on the same phone. Those modes work fully offline.

Permissions

The app uses internet access only for online play. It does not request access to your camera, microphone, contacts, location or files.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
