# Closed test — tester invitation (copy and send)

Send this to friends and family with Android phones. Collect 15–20 Gmail
addresses (Google requires at least 12 testers opted in for 14 days in a row).
The Gmail must be the account signed in to the Play Store on their Android
phone. iPhone users can't test.

Replace [OPT-IN LINK] with the link from Play Console → Testing → Closed
testing → Testers tab → "Copy link".

---

**Help me test my new game app 🎮**

Thanks for helping! It takes 2 minutes, and Google requires 14 days of
testing before my app can go public.

1. **Send me the Gmail address** you use on your Android phone (the one
   signed in to Play Store).
2. When I send you this link, **open it on your phone** and tap
   **"Become a tester"**: [OPT-IN LINK]
3. Tap **"Download it on Google Play"** and install **Graph Paper Games**
   like any normal app.
4. **Play a few times over the next 2 weeks** (any game, any mode) and send
   me any problems or ideas.
5. **Please don't leave the test or uninstall for 14 days.** It resets my
   countdown.

It's safe: installed from the official Play Store, no ads, no sign-up, no
permissions, no data collected. Android phones only for now.

## Why it's safe (if testers ask)

- Installed from the official Google Play Store; Google scans every app
  before testers can get it.
- No permissions requested (no contacts, camera, location or files). Internet
  is used only for online play with a friend.
- No accounts, no ads, no tracking, no data collected.
- No need to allow "install unknown apps" (that was only for the developer's
  own test file).
- The developer sees only the tester's email address (added to the list).
