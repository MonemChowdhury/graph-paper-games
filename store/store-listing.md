## Full description (max 4000 characters)
Three pen-and-paper classics, drawn on graph paper, in one small app.

TIC-TAC-TOE
Get three in a row before your opponent does. Quick rounds, perfect for a short break.

FOUR IN A ROW
Drop discs into the grid and line up four of your color across, down or diagonally.

DOTS & BOXES
Take turns drawing lines between dots. Close a box to claim it and draw again. Most boxes wins.

PLAY YOUR WAY
• Against the computer, on Easy or Hard
• Two players on the same phone, passing it back and forth
• Online with a friend anywhere: create a game, share the 5-letter code, and play live

NO STRINGS ATTACHED
• No sign-up or account
• No ads
• No tracking or data collection
• Small download, works offline (online play needs internet)

Players take turns going first after every round, and the scoreboard keeps track of who's ahead.
